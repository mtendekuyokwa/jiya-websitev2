## Design Context

### Users
Carpoolers looking to save money, who need reliable carpool partners to share rides and reduce transportation costs.

### Brand Personality
Fun, playful, and social, aiming to evoke confidence and delight in users.

### Aesthetic Direction
Organic visual direction, inspired by Uber, avoiding overly complex interfaces. Dark mode with a preference for yellow color.

### Design Principles
1. **User-Centric**: Design with the user in mind, ensuring a seamless and enjoyable experience.
2. **Confidence-Building**: Create an interface that instills trust and reliability.
3. **Accessibility-First**: Ensure the design meets WCAG AAA standards, including support for reduced motion and color blindness.
4. **Organic Aesthetics**: Use natural and organic elements to create a visually appealing and engaging interface.
5. **Simplicity**: Keep the design clean and uncluttered to avoid overwhelming users.
