# Jiya

## Register
brand

## Product
Jiya is a carpooling app for Malawi. Drivers with empty seats post trips between cities (Lilongwe, Blantyre, Mzuzu, Zomba and beyond); passengers find a seat going their way and split the cost. Operated by Mulinda Enterprises. Android today, iOS coming.

This website is the company's front door: it should read like a company with people and a place behind it, not a SaaS landing page.

## Users
Carpoolers looking to save money: students going home, workers commuting between cities, drivers who want fuel covered. They need reliable partners and confidence that the stranger in the car is trustworthy.

## Brand Personality
Fun, playful and social. It should evoke confidence and delight. Warm, local, human.

## Anti-references
- SaaS landing pages: feature grids, hero metrics, "Start free" CTAs, gradient glows, device-mockup tours.
- Corporate stock "diverse team high-fiving" imagery.
- Over-complex interfaces.

## Design Principles
1. User-centric: every section answers a carpooler's real question.
2. Confidence-building: real people, real routes, plain words about safety.
3. Accessibility-first: WCAG AAA contrast for body text, reduced motion respected, never rely on colour alone.
4. Organic aesthetics: rounded, natural shapes; photography of people over UI chrome.
5. Simplicity: one idea per section, uncluttered.

## Visual direction
The mobile app's palette (jiya-mobile/lib/tokens/colors.dart): deep teal primary, amber accents, warm sand ground, near-black dark ground. Light and dark mode, following the OS with a manual toggle. Libre Franklin throughout, large headings tracked tight like the app. Airy, borderless, storytelling layout in the spirit of YC's 2026 homepage redesign: people first, headlines that state the fact, motion only where it explains.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AAA target, prefers-reduced-motion support, colour-blind safe (no meaning carried by colour alone).
